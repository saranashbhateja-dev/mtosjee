Jee OS 2.1.1 — Duration refresh persistence fix

Fixed:
- When the app loads a previously saved browser state, fresh avgSeconds, totalSeconds,
  fetchedCount, and durationFetchedAt values from playlist-durations.json are now merged
  into matching saved chapters.
- The merge is metadata-only: lecture completion dates, chapter notes, custom chapter data,
  revisions, test history, settings, and saved links are not replaced by source defaults.
- Cached today's schedule is invalidated at startup so the planner recalculates using the
  refreshed duration metadata.
- New/empty local states are persisted after seeding or a duration refresh.

Preserved:
- 129 chapters and current lesson counts from the uploaded source.
- Backend-only YouTube API key handling.
- 1.5x estimated playback speed, six subject slots per study day, and whole-video scheduling.

Known source-data issues not guessed or silently altered:
- Physics chapter PLP26, “Conductors in Electrostatics,” has no valid playlist URL in the
  supplied source, so its durations cannot be fetched until the correct URL is supplied.
- PLP35 and PLC19 have tracker lesson counts that differ from listed YouTube playlist counts.
- The planner uses each playlist's average video duration as an estimate for each lesson; it
  does not have individual per-video durations in the current data format.
